Amulya is a PhD student in the Computational and Systems Biology program at MIT, co-advised by Eric Sun and Michael Segel. She is interested in novel computational and experimental approaches to measure, model, and reverse brain aging. Amulya received her AB in Computer Science at Harvard College, where she conducted research on 3D chromatin conformation with Bo Xia and on cell morphology with Jason Buenrostro. She also worked on cryopreservation at Until Labs. Outside of the lab, she enjoys yoga and writing.
