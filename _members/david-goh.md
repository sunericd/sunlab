David is a PhD student in the Harvard-MIT Health Sciences and Technology (HST) program. He develops multimodal generative AI to study how cellular states change across the lifespan and respond to perturbations, in search of targets for rejuvenation. He earned his MEng in Chemical Engineering from Imperial College London in 2025, including a year abroad at MIT. His previous work at Imperial, MIT, and MGH applied physics and machine learning to therapeutic design, gene regulation, immunology, and the microbiome. Outside the lab, David can often be found running along the Charles and window-shopping on Newbury Street.
